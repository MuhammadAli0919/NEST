01 / Academic partnership

Community thinking.
AIT excellence.

NES-Tribe is associated with Aligarh Institute of Technology, a Karachi polytechnic established in 1989. The connection expands access to technical learning, practical workshops and community-led engineering.

AIT is recognized for DAE technical diplomas under the Sindh Board of Technical Education and carries forward the Aligarh Movement values of unity, tolerance and academic excellence.

Who can join NES-Tribe?

Anyone with a genuine interest in engineering, science or their intersections. Students, educators, researchers and working professionals are all welcome.

Do I need to be an expert to join a tribe?

No. Tribes are built around shared curiosity, not credentials. We welcome first-time makers alongside experienced researchers.

How is NES-Tribe associated with AIT?

The community works alongside Aligarh Institute of Technology, connecting NESTribe projects, events and discussion with AIT's technical education environment in Karachi.

Where is the AIT campus?

Aligarh Institute of Technology is at Plot ST-16, Block 5, University Road, opposite Aziz Bhatti Park, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi.
